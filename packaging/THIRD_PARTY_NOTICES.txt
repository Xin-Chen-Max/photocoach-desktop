PhotoCoach Windows distribution - third-party components

This folder includes a Python runtime, PySide6/Qt and shiboken6, NumPy, rawpy
and LibRaw, Pillow and its imaging libraries, tifffile, and the PyInstaller
bootloader. Upstream license and copyright texts are copied under licenses/;
installed package metadata can also contain licenses in _internal/.

PySide6 and Qt are available under LGPLv3/GPL/commercial terms as described in
their included licenses. The LGPL libraries remain separate dynamic libraries
in this folder so they can be replaced with compatible modified versions.
Upstream Qt and PySide sources and build instructions:
https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/
https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git/
https://doc.qt.io/qtforpython-6/licenses.html

rawpy: https://github.com/letmaik/rawpy
LibRaw sources: https://www.libraw.org/download
NumPy: https://github.com/numpy/numpy
Pillow: https://github.com/python-pillow/Pillow
tifffile: https://github.com/cgohlke/tifffile
Python: https://www.python.org/downloads/source/
PyInstaller bootloader: https://github.com/pyinstaller/pyinstaller

No Adobe binaries, camera profiles, user photos, credentials, Ollama engine,
or AI model weights are included.
